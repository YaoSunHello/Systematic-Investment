# Deliverables Reference

Use these formats when the user asks for a concrete output. Keep labels explicit: facts, estimates, assumptions, model output, and judgement.

## Portfolio Risk Review

Recommended sections:

1. Mandate and constraints
2. Current exposures
3. Intended alpha bets
4. Unintended factor exposures
5. Position-sizing review
6. Liquidity, borrow, and turnover
7. Stress scenarios
8. Recommended actions
9. Open data gaps

Include a compact table when holdings data is available:

| Exposure | Current | Limit | Intended? | Concern | Action |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |

## Position Sizing Memo

Recommended sections:

1. Thesis and horizon
2. Expected return or conviction basis
3. Volatility, event risk, and liquidity
4. Correlation with existing book
5. Factor exposure created by the trade
6. Sizing recommendation
7. Kill criteria and review date

Useful output line:

`Recommended size: X% capital / Y% active risk contribution, subject to liquidity and factor limits.`

## Performance Review

Recommended sections:

1. Headline result
2. Factor attribution
3. Idiosyncratic attribution
4. Selection, sizing, and timing
5. Concentration and diversification effects
6. Trading costs and implementation
7. Lessons for the next rebalance

Avoid vague conclusions like "stock selection was good." Specify which names, sectors, themes, or catalysts contributed and whether the position sizes matched conviction.

## Hedge Recommendation

Recommended sections:

1. Exposure to hedge
2. Why the exposure is unintended or excessive
3. Candidate hedges
4. Hedge ratio and residual basis risk
5. Cost, liquidity, financing, and operational issues
6. Conditions for removing the hedge

Do not present a hedge as free. Every hedge has basis risk, cost, lost upside, or operational complexity.

## Alternative Data Assessment

Recommended sections:

1. Economic intuition
2. Coverage and history
3. Timestamp discipline and lookahead risk
4. Survivorship, selection, and vendor bias
5. Link to tradable forecast
6. Incremental value beyond existing signals
7. Capacity, decay, and implementation cost
8. Monitoring plan

Reject alternative data that cannot be connected to a falsifiable investment process.
