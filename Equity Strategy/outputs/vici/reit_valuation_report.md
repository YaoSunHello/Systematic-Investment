## Valuation

The midpoint of 2026 AFFO guidance is $2.455 per share. At $26.09, VICI trades at 10.6x forward AFFO and a 9.4% AFFO yield.

| Case | Assumption | Fair Value | Upside / Downside |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | 10.0x 2026E AFFO | $24.55 | -5.9% |
| Base | 12.0x 2026E AFFO | $29.46 | 12.9% |
| Bull | 13.5x 2026E AFFO | $33.14 | 27.0% |

Dividend support is meaningful. The $1.80 annualized dividend is covered at roughly 73% of 2026E AFFO midpoint, leaving a reinvestment buffer. A conservative dividend-discount check using next-year dividend of $1.87, 9.5% cost of equity, and 3.0% long-term growth gives about $28.80. A less conservative 9.0% cost of equity and 3.25% growth gives about $32.60. This supports a fair-value range around $29-$33.

## Investment View

VICI screens attractively for an income-plus-growth REIT position. The valuation is not distressed, but the combination of a high covered yield, mid-single-digit AFFO/share growth, investment-grade balance sheet, and contractual rent escalators supports an Accumulate call when priced around 10.5x-11.0x forward AFFO.

The stock is not a clean "Buy" under the project recommendation thresholds because the base upside is below 20%. It fits the "Moderate Buy / Accumulate" bucket: fair value is above market, income return is high, but the margin of safety is not large enough to ignore macro-rate and tenant concentration risk.

## Main Risks

- Interest rates: REIT multiples remain sensitive to the 10-year Treasury yield, which was around 4.5% on July 10, 2026.
- Leverage: 5.0x LQA net leverage is manageable, but refinancing cost matters because total debt is over $17 billion.
- Tenant and industry concentration: gaming real estate is durable but still tied to casino operator health and discretionary spending.
- External growth execution: VICI's growth depends partly on deploying capital into acquisitions and loans at attractive spreads.
- Methodology risk: AFFO is non-GAAP and company-defined. It should be checked against recurring capex, lease economics, and credit-loss adjustments.
